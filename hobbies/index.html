Hobbies

Guitar

I started playing classical music on a nylon string guitar in the 3rd grade. My preferred play style now is classic rock. My biggest inspirations are David Gilmour (Pink Floyd), Chad Smith (RHCP), Eric Clapton, Christine McVie (Fleetwood Mac), and more.

Climbing

I climb both indoors and outdoors. As of recently, I have been working on my bouldering strength, but I enjoy rope climbing as well. I'm inspired by the smooth style and perseverance of Tommy Caldwell, Dave Macleod, and Lonnie Kosuko Kauk.

Long Distance Running / Road Biking

I ran cross country competitively in high school. A lot of my training was done in the hills, and ever since I have enjoyed exploring new trails and roads in the mountains. This picture is of San Felipe hills in San Jose, California.
